## 1. [LOW] [PRECISION] The note for Cameron understates PR45: the reverse direction also lifts a failure on a DIFFERENT field of the other item

What is wrong. `accommodationCovers` (plan ~16897-16902) returns true for a
failed Pets or Evictions key whenever an accommodation sits on
`qualifications`. The pinned case "an accommodation on qualifications covers
a failed no_evictions_on_program" proves it. Here is the case: a property
with "No pets" written only in qualifications and the
`no_evictions_on_program` checkbox checked, and a tenant with an assistance
animal AND an eviction. The rubric (the reconciled line ~18177) tells the
model to raise the accommodation on `qualifications` alone and to report
`no_evictions_on_program` failed. `capOf` then lifts the eviction cap, even
though the eviction rule is a separate checkbox, not text inside
qualifications.

The PR45 / R3F2 entry for Cameron says only "an unrelated failure stated in
the same qualifications text". That is narrower than the code.

Correction to my round 3. I wrote that option (a) "adds little
over-suppression beyond what PR41 accepted". That holds for the forward
direction (Pets or Evictions key -> failed qualifications). It does not hold
for the reverse, which reaches other fields.

Why no decision change. Dropping the reverse would cap again an
assistance-animal tenant whose model flagged only `qualifications` while the
No pets box is also checked: the harm PR45 exists to remove. Every error
left falls toward not capping. The reason against is still given, and the
model is still told to score the pair Weak.

Fix. Change the PR45 / R3F2 line for Cameron to: "an accommodation raised on
qualifications lifts the cap for every Pets and Evictions requirement, on
any field, and an accommodation on a Pets or Evictions key lifts any failed
qualifications - always toward not capping."

## 2. [LOW] [PRECISION] RUNBOOK's "could not finish" entry names one cause; `MSG_ERROR` is also the catch-all

What is wrong. The new entry says "The ranking could not finish" means the
generation check failed to read the job record twice. But `startClaimedJob`
also finishes the job with `MSG_ERROR` on ANY unexpected throw ("ranking job
failed unexpectedly", plan ~25523-25527). The test "an assemble that throws
fails the job with the plain message" pins it (plan ~25170-25173).

What it implies. An operator sent to "check DynamoDB first" may chase the
wrong cause.

Fix. Add "or any unexpected error in the job; the worker's error line says
which ('generation check failed twice' or 'ranking job failed
unexpectedly')."

## 3. [LOW] [PRECISION] RUNBOOK and its cross-check grep miss the omitted-candidates message

What is wrong. S5's `omittedMessage` ("N tenants were not scored by the
model. Press Rank again.", plan ~23555-23558; tested at ~24991, ~24998) is a
job message staff will see. The R3B2 entries do not list it, and the S8.8
cross-check grep does not include "not scored by the model". The first
bullet covers the ROW ("the model did not return it") but not the job
message.

Fix. Add one bullet: the model left those candidates out of an answer that
otherwise parsed; their old matches stay; press Rank again. Add "not scored
by the model" to the grep.
