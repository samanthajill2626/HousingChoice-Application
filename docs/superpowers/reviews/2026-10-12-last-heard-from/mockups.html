Where should the "waiting on a reply" list live?

Every property sent to a tenant will get a status: No reply yet, Replied, Interested, or Not interested. One list will show the sends still waiting on a reply. The three mockups below show the same five tenants in three different places. The names and properties are examples.

The rules Option A - Matching page Option B - Today page Option C - on each property What every property shows Five questions

The rules, in plain words

  • Last heard from is the last time the tenant texted, called or emailed us, in any thread (one-to-one, relay or group). Texts we send, including blasts and reminders, do not count.
  • Replied means the tenant was heard from after the property was sent, in any thread. It does not say what they said; it says go read the thread.
  • Interested is one click that creates the tour request, so the tenant lands on Needs booking.
  • Not interested is one click. That tenant then shows as "Not interested" when the same property is matched again, and comes unchecked.
  • A send shows on the waiting list after 2 days of quiet (proposed), and drops off 14 days after the send (proposed). Inactive and Placed tenants never show.
  • Blasts and one-to-one sends are treated the same.

What every property shows, whichever option wins

The "Sent to" list on each property, and "Properties sent" on each tenant, show the same rows: when it was sent, when the tenant was last heard from, the status, and the two buttons. Interested creates the tour request and the row switches to "Tour requested". Not interested can be undone. A caseworker row has Not interested and a "Request a tour" link, since a caseworker's yes is a yes for one of their clients.

On the Tenants tab, each tenant will also show when they were last heard from, with the most recent first, and the Inactive and Placed tenants hidden unless turned on. On the Matching page, the Review recipients list shows each tenant's status, housing authority and last heard from, most recent first, and anyone who said no to that property shows unchecked with "Not interested".

Five questions

Where should the list live: A, B or C? (A and C can both be done.)Proposed: A, the Follow up tab on Matching.
How long after a send with no reply should a tenant appear?Proposed: 2 days of quiet. A tenant who replied on day 1 and then went quiet appears on day 3.
How far back should it look?Proposed: 14 days. A send older than two weeks drops off the list, even with no reply.
Should a tenant who replied and then went quiet show too, or only tenants who never replied?Proposed: yes, shown as Replied, since "replied, then went quiet" is the usual case.
Is anything missing from a row?Proposed: name, property, sent date, last heard from, status, Interested, Not interested, Text.
